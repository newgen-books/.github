# NewGen Books

**Great books for growth, in English at your level — [books.dev](https://books.dev)**

NewGen Books is a free online library for people who grow by reading. The shelf holds the books that shaped how we think — novels, philosophy, essays and memoirs, from *Pride and Prejudice* to *Walden* and the *Tao Te Ching* — in the original English and in simpler editions you can actually finish.

No app to install and no sign-up needed to start: open a book in the browser on your phone or computer and read.

## What's on the site

- **80+ great books, free.** Classic novels, philosophy, essays, memoirs and fables from the public domain, free to read with no limits.
- **Editions for your level.** Many books also come in simpler editions for B1–C1 readers, so the ideas reach you before the vocabulary stops you.
- **A measured CEFR level for every edition.** Each version shows how hard it really is, measured on the text itself. *Alice* goes from C1.2 in the original to B2.1 and B1.5 in the adapted editions.
- **Tap any word.** One tap shows the translation, pronunciation and a plain-English explanation — without leaving the page.
- **Listen like an audiobook.** Every book has its own narrator: North American or British, male or female, at any speed, with each word highlighted as it is read.
- **Words that stick.** Save new words and they turn into flashcards that come back right before you'd forget them.
- **Know your level.** A five-minute vocabulary test measures where you are, so you can pick the right edition.
- **Bring your own book.** Upload a book you love and read it with the same translations, audio and review.
- **See yourself grow.** Books finished, words learned and your daily streak.

## Why it's different

| Usual way | NewGen Books |
|---|---|
| Great books stay on the "someday" list because the English feels too hard | Each book comes in an edition at your level, with help on every page |
| Graded readers are paid and usually heavily abridged | The original book and its simpler editions side by side, free |
| A level on the cover is a publisher's guess | The level is measured on the text, edition by edition |
| Dictionary in one tab, audiobook in another app, flashcards in a third | Reading, translation, audio and review on one page |

## Who it's for

- Readers who want to grow with the great books in English: novels, philosophy, essays and memoirs.
- English learners from B1 to C1 who want real books, not exercises.
- Students preparing for IELTS or study abroad who need more reading practice.
- Teachers looking for free extensive-reading material at a known level.

The interface is available in English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Czech and Finnish.
